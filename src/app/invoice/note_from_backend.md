| Order | Setup                     | Why it comes first                                                                                                                         |
| ----- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1** | Chart of Accounts         | Everything financial ultimately posts to GL accounts. Other records often reference these accounts.                                        |
| **2** | Company Bank Accounts     | Payments cannot be made without knowing which company account will fund them.                                                              |
| **3** | Payment Terms             | Purchase Orders and Vendor Bills need default payment terms.                                                                               |
| **4** | Vendors                   | Purchase Orders must reference a vendor. Subcontractor requests may already have a vendor or create one.                                   |
| **5** | Vendor Bank Accounts      | Needed before actual payment processing. A vendor can exist without a bank account initially, but payment cannot be completed without one. |
| **6** | Request Mapping           | Defines how each approved Project Request type is converted into Invoice transactions.                                                     |
| **7** | Purchase Order Generation | Convert approved Purchase and Plant & Equipment requests into Purchase Orders.                                                             |
| **8** | Vendor Bills              | Record invoices received from vendors.                                                                                                     |
| **9** | Payments                  | Process payments and update the ledger.                                                                                                    |
